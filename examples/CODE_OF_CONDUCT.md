# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Eye of Horus is committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. This pledge applies to every project space — the issue tracker, pull requests, RFC discussions, community channels, and any events organized under the project's name.

This code of conduct is written in the spirit of, and closely aligned with, the widely adopted Contributor Covenant framework used across the open-source community; contributors familiar with that framework will recognize its structure and expectations here.

## Our Standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- being respectful and precise, especially when disagreeing about technical claims;
- critiquing ideas and arguments rather than the people who hold them;
- distinguishing speculation from evidence, and saying plainly which one you are offering — a norm that mirrors the project's own documentation standards in [`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`](DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md);
- helping newcomers understand the research status of the project so they can calibrate their expectations accurately;
- gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and offering it in a form that can be acted on.

Behavior that will not be tolerated includes:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form;
- personal attacks, insulting or derogatory comments, and sustained disruption of discussion;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- deliberately misrepresenting the project's implementation status in a way that contradicts [Design Principle 1](DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md#1-honesty-before-spectacle) after being corrected;
- any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces — for example, when using an official project email address, posting from an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at **contact@agentflow-enterprise.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with the reporter's privacy and safety respected throughout.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct in good faith may face consequences as determined by other members of the project's governance structure, as it matures (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow a proportionate response to violations, generally in the following order, though severity may warrant skipping directly to a later step:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including temporary restriction from interaction with the project for a specified period.
3. **Temporary Ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period.
4. **Permanent Ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.
